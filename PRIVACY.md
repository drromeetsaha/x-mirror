# Privacy policy: x-mirror

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

x-mirror is a personal tool run by Dr. Romeet Saha. It copies posts from his
own X account (@DrRomeetSaha) to his own Facebook Page (*The ChartRoom*) and his
own Instagram account (@dr.romeetsaha). It is not offered to anyone else, and
nobody can sign in to it.

## What data it uses

- **X:** the text and media of posts published by @DrRomeetSaha. When one of
  those posts quotes another public post, it also uses the quoted post's text
  and its author's public name and handle.
- **Facebook and Instagram:** access to The ChartRoom Page and @dr.romeetsaha,
  used only to publish those posts.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect, store or process data about any other Facebook,
  Instagram or X users.
- It does not use cookies, tracking or analytics.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.

## Storage

The only thing it stores is a small progress file, `state.json`, in this
repository. That file holds the IDs of posts already copied, plus the text of
any post still waiting to be published.

## Data deletion

Nothing about other people is stored, so there is no other data to delete.

- To remove x-mirror's access to Facebook or Instagram, go to **Facebook →
  Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations** and remove
  **x-mirror**.
- For questions or deletion requests, message **The ChartRoom** Page on
  Facebook.
